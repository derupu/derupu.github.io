What I can provide

I tailor each report to what you want emphasized, such as how the game feels, what players may get confused on, as well as where people may want to stop playing. Tell me your focus and I’ll structure the feedback around it.

Bug reports

Each report names the build and platform, rates severity and how often the bug happens, lists the steps to reproduce it, and compares expected and actual results, with screenshots or video timecodes as evidence. Developers can reproduce an issue quickly and decide what to fix first.

Gameplay recordings with commentary

I record in high quality with OBS or NVIDIA recording and talk through what I’m doing. You hear bugs as they happen, where menus or mechanics confuse me, how performance holds up, and what I’d change.

Written feedback

Notes on gameplay and mechanics, controls, UI and UX, performance and stability, readability and accessibility, tutorials and onboarding, progression and difficulty, and the overall player experience.

Kradle.ai AI mini games: feedback report

I tested the BuildWithAI and Skyblock mini games and wrote up gameplay, mechanics and AI behavior for each.

Bugs found

  • Sessions ended early and sent me back to the hub, mostly during complex build tasks.
  • AI players left before the session timer ran out.
  • AI players forgot restrictions I gave them earlier, such as no slime blocks or observers.

Suggestions

  • A cleaner chat format when several AI players talk at once.
  • A live display of what each AI is doing, with a cancel option.

Each bug in the full report has steps to reproduce and expected versus actual results.

Back returns to the pause menu.

About me

I love games, and I’m just as curious about how they work as I am about playing them. Finding a bug, working out how to trigger it every time, and writing it up so a developer can fix it is the part of playtesting I enjoy most.

I’m happy to test any genre on any platform, and I notice the small things, like a menu that takes one tap too many or a tutorial that explains too little. I share findings in writing and in recorded commentary, and I keep working to get better at both.
